3 · Shape the sound

Feel - why a good sample still sounds like MIDI

Karplus-Strong only

clip is note length (1 = the full beat); lpf rolls off the top - the quickest cure for a brittle, cheap-keyboard edge. release is what stops a note being cut off dead when a rest follows.
strum spreads a chord's notes instead of firing them together; detune puts each string a stable few cents out, the way a real one is; bass ring lets the lowest note of a voicing keep sounding under the rest; fret noise squeaks just before the downbeat, where the app changes shape.
